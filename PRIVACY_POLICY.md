# Privacy Policy

See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md) for the complete, canonical privacy policy and data-handling specification.

## Summary
- **Local-First**: Decision records (`.decisions/*.md`) remain entirely local to your Git repository.
- **Opt-In Auto-Capture**: Transcripts are processed only if you explicitly run `ideagit consent`.
- **Local Redaction**: Known credentials, API keys, tokens, and private keys are scrubbed before any transcript leaves your machine.
- **Zero Telemetry**: IdeaGit does not collect or transmit usage analytics.
